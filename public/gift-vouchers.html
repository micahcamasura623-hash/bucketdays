Gift vouchers

Give an experience instead of another thing. Choose an amount and we'll email you a code straight after payment. Forward it to whoever it's for.

Choose an amount

Secure payment by Stripe. You'll get the voucher code by email within a minute or two of paying.

How it works

  1. Buy a voucher and receive a code by email.
  2. Forward the email to the person you're giving it to, or print it.
  3. They choose any experience on BucketDays, pick a date, and enter the code before paying.

Good to know

  • Valid for 12 months from the date of purchase.
  • Works on any activity. If the booking costs more, they pay the difference. If it costs less, the rest stays on the voucher.
  • Can't be exchanged for cash.
  • If you change your mind, you can cancel an unused voucher within 14 days for a full refund. See our terms and refund policy.
