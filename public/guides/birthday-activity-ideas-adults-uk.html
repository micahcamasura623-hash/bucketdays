Birthday activities for a group

For a group of friends, pick something where everyone is doing it together rather than watching. Axe throwing and karaoke are easy to turn into a friendly competition. Rage buggies and off-road karting work well when people like a bit of racing. A cocktail masterclass (from £97) or gin tasting (from £63) suits a calmer crowd. Check each activity page for group sizes and rules.

Things to do for a birthday in November and winter

A winter birthday doesn't have to mean a pub. Several of these are indoors, so the weather doesn't matter: indoor skydiving, bouldering, karaoke, pottery, a spa day, a comedy night and a cocktail masterclass. Outdoor activities like skydiving and ballooning can be rearranged if the weather is bad, so book with a bit of flexibility.

Doing your birthday on your own?

Birthdays don't need a group. Skydiving, indoor skydiving, rage buggies, clay pigeon shooting, flying lessons and evening cruises all welcome solo bookings, and you're usually paired with an instructor anyway. See our guide to things to do in London on your own.

A few practical tips

  • Book midweek if you can. Many venues are quieter, and you get more attention from the instructor.
  • Check the weather policy for outdoor activities. Rescheduling is common.
  • Build in flexibility. Don't plan something straight after a weather-dependent activity.
  • Check age, weight and fitness rules on each activity page before you book.
  • Our refund policy gives a full refund up to 14 days before your date.

Frequently asked questions

What is a good birthday activity for an adult?

It depends on the vibe. For adrenaline, a rage buggy or indoor skydiving. For something memorable but calmer, an evening cruise or a trial flying lesson. For a skill, a drum lesson or clay pigeon shooting. All welcome complete beginners.

What are fun birthday activities for adults in November?

Indoor ones are the safest bet in winter: indoor skydiving, bouldering, karaoke, pottery, a spa day, a comedy night or a cocktail masterclass. Outdoor activities can be rescheduled if the weather turns.

What are good birthday ideas for a group?

Axe throwing, karaoke, rage buggies, off-road karting and a cocktail masterclass all work well for groups because everyone takes part. Check each activity page for group sizes.

What can you do for a birthday on your own?

Plenty. Most experiences welcome solo bookings, including tandem skydiving, indoor skydiving, rage buggies, clay pigeon shooting, flying lessons and evening cruises.

How much should you spend on a birthday activity?

You can have a great birthday experience from around £35 to £50. Mid-range sits around £65 to £115, while bucket-list activities like a tandem skydive or flying lesson run from £240 to £290.
