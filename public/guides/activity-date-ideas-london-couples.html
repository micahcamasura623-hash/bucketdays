Activity Date Ideas for Couples in London

A BucketDays guide · Updated 2026

Dinner and drinks are fine, but an activity gives you something to do together and something to talk about. Here are date ideas for couples, from relaxed to adventurous. Prices are "from" prices per person, so check the activity page for the current figure.

Relaxed and romantic

  • Thames evening cruise — from £37. See the sights from the water as it gets dark.
  • London Eye — from £32. A 30 minute ride with a view across the city.
  • Spa day — from £89. A slow day together.
  • Hot air balloon flight — from £150. A special occasion gift for two.

Hands-on and creative

  • Pottery wheel class — from £48. You will both end up covered in clay. It is easy to laugh about.
  • Cocktail masterclass — from £45. Make your own drinks.
  • Gin tasting — from £42. A calm way to try something new together.

Adventurous

  • Indoor skydiving — from £55. Take turns in the wind tunnel.
  • Rage buggy — from £70. A little competition on an off-road track.
  • Axe throwing — from £30. Easy to learn and good for banter.
  • Tandem skydive — from £230. For couples who want the big one.

A few things to check

  • For a first date, pick something where you can talk. A cruise, a cocktail class or pottery all work.
  • If one of you is nervous, pick something gentle. Adrenaline dates are great, but only if you both want them.
  • Check the age and weight rules before you book flying or adrenaline activities.
  • Our refund policy gives a full refund up to 14 days before the date.

Frequently asked questions

What is a good first date activity in London?

Something with time to talk, such as a Thames evening cruise, a cocktail masterclass or a pottery class.

What is a cheap date idea in London?

Bouldering, karaoke, a comedy night or axe throwing all start between £18 and £30 per person.

What is a special date idea for an anniversary?

A hot air balloon flight, a spa day or a Thames evening cruise are popular for anniversaries.
