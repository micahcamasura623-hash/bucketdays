Experience Gifts Under £50 in the UK

A BucketDays guide · Updated 2026

You do not need to spend a lot to give someone a good day out. These experiences all start at £50 or less, and most are fine for complete beginners. Prices are "from" prices per person and match what you see when you book.

Nights out

  • Comedy club night — from £35. Good for a night out, with friends or alone.
  • Karaoke private room — from £38. Your own room, great for a group.
  • Thames evening cruise — from £45. A relaxed, scenic option for couples.

Active and creative

  • Drum taster lesson — from £30. Good for the person who always wanted to try.
  • London Eye — from £32. A 30 minute ride with a view across the city.
  • Bouldering — from £40. Easy to learn and no ropes.

Slightly higher, up to £50

  • Axe throwing — from £50. Simple to pick up and good for groups.

A few things to check

  • Look at the length of the session. Some of these last about an hour, others two to three hours.
  • For gifts to a group, karaoke, axe throwing and cocktails work well.
  • Most of these welcome solo visitors, so you do not have to go along.
  • Our refund policy gives a full refund up to 14 days before the date.

Frequently asked questions

What is the cheapest experience gift?

The drum taster lesson is the cheapest on our list at from £30, followed by the London Eye at from £32 and a comedy club night at from £35.

What is a good experience gift under £50 for a couple?

A Thames evening cruise at from £45 works well for two. Comedy and karaoke nights are good choices too.

Are these suitable for beginners?

Yes. Bouldering, axe throwing and drum lessons all work for people who have never tried them. Check the activity page for any age or fitness rules.
