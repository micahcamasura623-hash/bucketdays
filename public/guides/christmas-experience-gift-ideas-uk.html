Christmas Experience Gift Ideas in the UK

A BucketDays guide · Updated 2026

Most people already have enough stuff. An experience gives them something to look forward to and a story to tell afterwards. Here are experience gift ideas for Christmas 2026, sorted by budget. Prices are "from" prices per person and match what you see when you book.

Under £50

  • Drum taster lesson — from £30. Good for the person who always wanted to try.
  • London Eye — from £32. A 30 minute ride with a view across the city.
  • Comedy club night — from £35. Good for a night out, with friends or alone.
  • Karaoke private room — from £38. Your own room, great for a group.
  • Bouldering — from £40. Easy to learn and no ropes.
  • Thames evening cruise — from £45. A relaxed, scenic option for couples.

£50 to £120

  • Axe throwing — from £50. Simple to pick up and good for groups.
  • Kayaking taster — from £53. A good first go at kayaking.
  • Gin tasting — from £63. For people who like their drinks.
  • Indoor skydiving — from £65. Freefall without the plane.
  • Off-road karting — from £70. Muddy, fast and good fun.
  • Paddleboarding — from £70. A calm way to be on the water.
  • Rage buggy — from £80. Throw a buggy round an off-road track, no licence needed.
  • Pottery wheel class — from £95. A creative gift where they take something home.
  • Cocktail masterclass — from £97. Learn a few drinks, then drink them.
  • Clay pigeon shooting — from £110. Popular with people who have everything.
  • Spa day — from £115. The classic gift for someone who needs a rest.

Bucket-list gifts, £240 and up

  • Aeroplane trial flying lesson — from £240. They take the controls with an instructor beside them.
  • Hot air balloon flight — from £250. Slow, quiet and memorable.
  • Tandem skydive — from £290. The biggest gift on the list.
  • Helicopter trial lesson — from £320. A rare thing to do for most people.

A few things to check

  • Pick something that matches the person, not what you would enjoy. A skydive is a bad gift for someone scared of heights.
  • Check age, weight and fitness rules on flying and adrenaline activities before you buy.
  • Outdoor activities can be rearranged for weather, so choose a date with some flexibility.

Frequently asked questions

What is a good experience gift for Christmas?

It depends on the person and the budget. For a small budget, try a comedy night, bouldering or karaoke. In the middle, a spa day, pottery class or clay pigeon shooting. For a big gift, a flying lesson, balloon flight or tandem skydive.

How much should I spend on an experience gift?

You can give a good experience from around £30 to £45. Mid-range gifts cost about £50 to £120. Bucket-list flights and skydives cost from £240 to £320.

Can I book an experience for someone else?

Yes. You can pick the activity and date, then share the details with the person. Check each activity page for the rules, such as age and weight limits.
