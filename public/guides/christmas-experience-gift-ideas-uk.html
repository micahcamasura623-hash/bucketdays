Christmas Experience Gift Ideas in the UK

A BucketDays guide · Updated 2026

Most people already have enough stuff. An experience gives them something to look forward to and a story to tell afterwards. Here are experience gift ideas for Christmas 2026, sorted by budget. Every price below is a "from" price, so check the activity page for the current figure.

Under £35

  • Bouldering — from £18. Cheap, active and good for first-timers.
  • Karaoke private room — from £20. A fun one for a group of friends.
  • Comedy club night — from £22. A good gift for someone who likes a night out.
  • Axe throwing — from £30. Surprisingly easy to pick up, and good for a group.
  • Off-road karting — from £30. The cheapest adrenaline gift on the list.

£35 to £100

  • Thames evening cruise — from £37. A relaxed, scenic option for couples.
  • Gin tasting — from £42. A good choice for people who like their drinks.
  • Cocktail masterclass — from £45. Learn to make a few drinks, then drink them.
  • Pottery wheel class — from £48. A creative gift where they take something home.
  • Clay pigeon shooting — from £50. Popular with people who say they have everything.
  • Indoor skydiving — from £55. Freefall without the plane.
  • Rage buggy — from £70. Throw a buggy around an off-road track, no licence needed.
  • Spa day — from £89. The classic gift for someone who needs a rest.

Bucket-list gifts, £150 and up

  • Hot air balloon flight — from £150. Slow, quiet and memorable.
  • Aeroplane trial flying lesson — from £165. They take the controls with an instructor beside them.
  • Tandem skydive — from £230. The biggest gift on the list.
  • Helicopter trial lesson — from £250. A rare thing to do for most people.

A few things to check

  • Pick something that matches the person, not what you would enjoy. A skydive is a bad gift for someone scared of heights.
  • Check age, weight and fitness rules on flying and adrenaline activities before you buy.
  • Outdoor activities can be rearranged for weather, so choose a date with some flexibility.

Frequently asked questions

What is a good experience gift for Christmas?

It depends on the person and the budget. For a small budget, try bouldering, karaoke or axe throwing. In the middle, a spa day, pottery class or clay pigeon shooting. For a big gift, a flying lesson, balloon flight or tandem skydive.

How much should I spend on an experience gift?

You can give a good experience from around £20 to £30. Mid-range gifts cost £50 to £90. Bucket-list flights and skydives cost from £150 to £250 or more.

Can I book an experience for someone else?

Yes. You can pick the activity and date, then share the details with the person. Check each activity page for the rules, such as age and weight limits.
